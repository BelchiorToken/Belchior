Belchior

Belchior (BEL) is a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain. The price in this paper is always BEL / USDC. The largest pool is BEL / XRP. The phone counts a mining score. Real BEL leaves the vault only when the same wallet starts mining on the contract and later claims.

The name

Belchior is a family name. The family dates the line to 1993, and its rule has been the same: work hard, save harder. BEL carries that name onto BNB Smart Chain. It is not a promise of profit. It is a fixed-supply token with a public contract, a public mining vault, and a phone app.

Mining

The Android app is the miner. Tap the B to start. Tap again to stop.

  • The base rate is 1 BEL per hour.
  • The score is saved on the phone. It is still there the next day.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes the score.
  • The score on the phone is not BEL in a wallet.

Wallets: Binance, Trust, and MetaMask, on BNB Smart Chain. A pasted address also works.

The owner can add BEL, up to the cap, by sending it and calling sync. The owner can withdraw BEL that has not already been mined. There is no minimum and no fee.

A claim pays only time that started on the contract. The same wallet calls startMine. Later it calls claim. The contract sends 100% of that mined amount to the wallet. A little BNB is required for the fee. If startMine was never confirmed, claim pays nothing.

The vault’s rate uses the BEL/USDT pair 0x1d7eedbb3da26c04ab1fb8756bf763e84d6370a4, about 805,729 BEL and 53.59 USDT. That pair is not the published price. The published price is BEL / USDC.

Staking

Staking is not live. It is not in the app and it is not in the vault. Mining is the only active score. A later staking contract would use its own BEL, not the 14,000 already in the mining vault.

What this paper does not say

  • It does not say the price will rise.
  • It does not say the phone score is already BEL you can sell.
  • It does not say staking is open.
  • Holding BEL can lose money. Read the contract before you buy.
