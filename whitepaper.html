Belchior

Belchior (BEL) is a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain. This paper replaces the earlier one. The old lock amounts, liquidity amounts, and dollar values were wrong. The numbers below are the ones on the chain.

THE NAME

Belchior is a family name. The family dates the line to 1993, and its rule has been the same: work hard, save harder. BEL is the token that carries that name onto BNB Smart Chain. It is not a promise of profit. It is a fixed-supply token with a public contract, a public mining vault, and a phone app that counts a mining score.

MINING

The Android app is the miner. Tap the B to start. Tap again to stop.

  • The base rate is 1 BEL per hour.
  • Once a day the app marks the BEL price. If the price is higher than that mark, the hour pays more than 1. If it is lower, the hour pays less. The rate does not go below zero.
  • The score is saved on the phone. It is still there the next day.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes the score.
  • The score is not BEL in a wallet. The app does not pay tokens yet.

Wallets: Binance, Trust, and MetaMask, on BNB Smart Chain. A pasted address also works. The next app build adds a QR code and buttons that open those wallets.

The owner can add more BEL, up to the cap, by sending BEL and calling sync. The owner can also withdraw BEL that has not already been mined. Mined amounts that a user has already earned are not taken back.

There is no PinkLock in this version. The earlier paper's 50,000,000 BEL PinkLock and 46,800,000 BEL treasury line are withdrawn. They were not the numbers on this contract.

WITHDRAWAL RULE

This is the rule for the version that pays. It is not enforced by the vault that holds the 14,000 BEL today. No claim button pays real BEL yet.

  • A user can withdraw only after 1,000 BEL have been mined.
  • The user receives 95%.
  • 5% stays in the same vault.
  • At 1 BEL per hour, 1,000 BEL is about 42 days of continuous mining.

Before the first real payout, the vault will be replaced with a contract that contains this rule. The 14,000 BEL can be moved into that contract by the owner.

FUTURE STAKING

Staking is not live. It is not in the app, and it is not in the vault. Mining stays the only active score.

When staking is added, it will be a separate choice from mining:

  • A holder locks BEL in a staking contract.
  • The rate is meant to be low, starting near 3% a year, and it can change with how much BEL is staked.
  • Staking rewards are not taken from the phone's mining score.
  • The mining vault is not automatically the staking vault. Staking gets its own supply, so the two do not spend the same 14,000 BEL twice.
  • Until that contract exists, any APY shown anywhere else is not Belchior staking.

HISTORY

  • Belchior was issued on BNB Smart Chain. The contract reports a supply of 100,000,000 BEL.
  • Two PancakeSwap V2 pairs were opened: BEL/USDT and BEL/XRP.
  • An earlier public note used the wrong liquidity and the wrong lock sizes. This paper replaces that note.
  • The Android app was built. It counts the mining score on the phone and stores it there.
  • The mining vault was deployed. 14,000 BEL was sent in and synced to the reward pool. The cap is 1,000,000 BEL.
  • The paying rules were set, and are waiting on the next contract: withdraw after 1,000 BEL mined, 5% stays in the vault.
  • Staking was left for a later contract.
