1. Introduction

Belchior Token (BEL) is a BEP-20 cryptocurrency deployed on BNB Chain with a focus on transparency, security and value preservation.

BEL is presented as a public, auditable digital asset. Visitors should independently verify all blockchain claims before making financial decisions.

2. Vision

BEL aims to emphasize transparent token distribution, publicly verifiable blockchain information and open documentation of relevant token activity.

The project intends to prioritize transparent information, liquidity development, community growth and potential future utility.
