HOW THE HOUR IS PAID

The base is 1 BEL per hour. Once a day the app marks the BEL price. A higher price raises the hour. A lower price cuts it. It does not go below zero.

  • Withdrawals open only after 1,000 BEL mined.
  • You would receive 95%. The 5% stays in the vault.
  • That is about 42 days of continuous mining.
  • Staking is later, and it is not this score.
